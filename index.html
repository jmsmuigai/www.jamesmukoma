Ongoing Projects in Garissa County

Applying the AURA ecosystem to solve critical, real-world challenges. These projects demonstrate the power of integrated GeoAI in action.

Project Sentinel: Flood & Drought EWS

Predictive AI using Sentinel data to create an early warning system for communities along the Tana River.

Tools: GEE, Gemini, Cloud Functions, Google Colab

Project Verdant: Rangeland & Livestock AI

Mapping rangeland health and developing an AI model to identify cattle by muzzle prints for enhanced management.

Tools: GEE, Vertex AI, Google Cloud Storage

Project TerraForm: Invasive Species AI

A GeoAI system to detect and monitor the invasive Mathenge plant and analyze its impact on water aquifers.

Tools: GEE, BigQuery, Looker Studio

Project Harvest: AI for Soil & Crop Yield

An attention-based CNN model that predicts soil nutrient gaps and crop productivity for farmers.

Tools: TensorFlow, Vertex AI, Colab

Project Shirika: Refugee Integration AI

Analyzing the Shirika plan with GeoAI to model resource allocation and social integration patterns for refugees.

Tools: GEE, BigQuery, Gemini, Looker Studio

Project Atlas: Garissa Living Atlas

Mapping Garissa County's living atlas layers to monitor rangeland degradation and ecosystem health over time.

Tools: GEE, Google Workspace, Looker Studio
